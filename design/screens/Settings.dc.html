Learning and explanations

Briefings, reviews and answers are written by the model from numbers computed in code. A check rejects any text with a number that isn't in the facts. Ask, briefings and reviews count toward the monthly cost cap.

Shadow strategies

A shadow runs on the same data every cycle and records what it would have done, with its own paper account. It never places orders. Each one roughly doubles model calls on cycles where it runs.

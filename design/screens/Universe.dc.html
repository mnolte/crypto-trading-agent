How the check works
  • Volume and spread come from the last 30 daily candles and Bitvavo's 24h ticker; the 300 daily candles are counted only for markets that pass both.
  • An eligible market loses its place after 3 failing days in a row, or at once when it is 20% past a line (under €0.8M volume) or no longer trading. It then needs 3 passing days in a row to return.
  • A failing active market stays active and takes no new entries until you deactivate it or it recovers. An open position is still managed.
  • If the check itself fails, the previous result stays and a banner says so.
